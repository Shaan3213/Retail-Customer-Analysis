# 📊 Retail Customer Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing retail transactional data using **Python** and **Pandas** to understand customer behavior, purchasing patterns, product popularity, and store performance.

The analysis follows a complete end-to-end data analysis workflow, including:

- Data Cleaning
- Data Validation
- Data Merging
- Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
- Business Insight Generation

The project was performed using multiple datasets containing customer information, transaction records, and product hierarchy details.

---

# 🎯 Business Objective

The objective of this project is to help the retail business analyze day-to-day transactions and generate meaningful business insights related to:

- Customer purchasing behavior
- Product category popularity
- Store performance
- Revenue trends
- Customer segmentation

The analysis supports data-driven decision-making for marketing, sales optimization, and customer targeting.

The following preprocessing steps were performed before analysis:

# 📈 Business Problems Solved

The project answers several retail business questions, including:

- Identifying the most popular product categories among male and female customers
- Finding the city with the maximum number of customers
- Determining which store type generates the highest sales and quantity
- Calculating revenue contribution from specific categories and customer segments
- Identifying customers with high transaction frequency
- Analyzing spending patterns of customers within specific age groups

---

# 🔑 Key Insights

- 📚 Books category was the most frequently purchased category among both male and female customers
- 🛒 e-Shop generated the highest revenue and quantity sold among all store types
- 🔄 Negative transaction amounts represented product returns/refunds
- 👥 Only a small number of customers had more than 10 unique positive transactions
- 💰 Electronics and Books categories contributed significantly to customer spending among the 25–35 age group
